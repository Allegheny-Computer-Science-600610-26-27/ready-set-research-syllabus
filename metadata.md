## Project Contract

A student who is majoring in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data
Science, or Informatics should adhere to all the requirements in the course
syllabus for their 600 and 610 courses. A student who is a double major must
adhere to all the published requirements for both of their majors. This
includes the stated requirements for their Computer Science, Software
Engineering, Data Science, or Informatics major and the requirements for their
additional major that is not in Computer and Information Science.

A student who is not a double major should provide:

```text
List any additional requirements for your senior comprehensive project in
Computer and Information Science, including any approved schedule changes,
modifications to the requirements stated in the syllabus, or regulations
concerning the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools.
```

A student who is a double major should provide:

```text
List any additional requirements for your senior comprehensive project in
Computer and Information Science, including any approved schedule changes,
modifications to the requirements stated in the syllabus, or regulations
concerning the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools. Further list any
requirements for the completion of your additional major, including details
about joint meetings, joint presentations, and any other requirements that are
not stated in the syllabus for the 600-level course in Computer and Information
Science.
```

A student should add an X-mark to the box to indicate that they have defined a
project contract and had either their first and second readers or, if they are
a double major, their two first readers approve it.

- [ ] I have written a project contract and had either my first and second
readers or my two first readers approve it.

## Checklist of Tasks

Use this checklist to confirm that all required metadata and project-contract
steps are complete.

- [ ] Enter my full name.
- [ ] Review the applicable `600`-level and `610`-level course syllabi.
- [ ] Confirm whether I am completing a single major or a double major.
- [ ] Complete the Honor Code pledge.
- [ ] Enter my GitHub username.
- [ ] Provide the GitHub repository URL for my senior thesis chapters and
  research journal.
- [ ] Provide the GitHub repository URL for my computational artifact.
- [ ] Confirm that both repositories are accessible to my readers and contain
  the expected project materials.
- [ ] Enter the title of my senior comprehensive project.
- [ ] Record any additional requirements for the Computer and Information
  Science project.
- [ ] Record any approved schedule changes or modifications to the syllabus
  requirements.
- [ ] Record any approved course-specific regulations concerning the use of
  artificial intelligence tools.
- [ ] If I am a double major, record the requirements for my additional major,
  including joint meetings, joint presentations, and other major-specific
  requirements.
- [ ] If I am not a double major and have no additional requirements, explicitly
  state that no additional requirements apply.
- [ ] Review the completed project contract with the appropriate readers.
- [ ] Obtain approval from my first and second readers or, for a double major,
  from both first readers.
- [ ] Perform a final review to confirm that all metadata, repository links,
  project details, and contract requirements are complete and accurate.
